## Optimization Rules

1. **Do not camp Slayer before DT1.** At 59 Slayer, normal Slayer is fine, but it does not solve the Magic grind efficiently yet.
2. **Do not train Magic to 62 before DT1.** DT1 requires only 50 Magic and rewards Magic XP, so training first wastes the reward timing.
3. **Do Path of Glouphrie before generic Slayer grinding.** It is required for WGS and gives 20k Slayer XP.
4. **Treat 65 Slayer as the Magic/Slayer engine breakpoint.** Dust devils require 65 Slayer and are the first major burst task.
5. **Keep passive skills running from day one.** Birdhouses and tree runs reduce the Hunter/Farming walls while you quest.

## Phase 1 — Compress the Route to Desert Treasure I

The priority is to unlock Ancient Magicks earlier than the original WGS-only route.

1. **Finish The Tourist Trap**
   - Put the reward XP into **Hunter**.
   - This is a direct DT1 prerequisite and helps the WGS Hunter wall.

2. **Do The Feud**
   - Gives 15k Thieving XP.
   - Unlocks blackjacking, which is the main Thieving method for the WGS wall.

3. **Blackjack Thieving to 53**
   - 53 Thieving is the DT1 requirement.
   - Keep going only if you are in a rhythm; Path of Glouphrie later needs 56, and WGS eventually needs 72.

4. **Complete the remaining DT1 quest blockers**
   - **Temple of Ikov**
   - **Death Plateau → Troll Stronghold**
   - Already done: The Dig Site, Priest in Peril, Waterfall Quest.

5. **Do Desert Treasure I immediately**
   - You already clear the Magic requirement.
   - This unlocks Ancient Magicks and pays Magic XP before you train toward 62.

Checkpoint: after this phase, you have Ancients unlocked, DT1 quest points banked, and the Magic grind can start feeding into Slayer.

## Phase 2 — Turn On the Magic/Slayer Engine

This is where the route changes from "WGS skilling" into "WGS plus useful combat progress."

1. **Train Magic to 62**
   - 62 unlocks **Smoke Burst**.
   - Best active option: high alch or tele-alch if you can tolerate it.
   - Best low-effort option: crabs.
   - Best cheap crab setup after Family Crest: **Fire Bolt + chaos gauntlets**.

2. **Do Family Crest when convenient**
   - Requirements: 40 Mining, 40 Smithing, 59 Magic, 40 Crafting.
   - You already clear Mining/Crafting; train Smithing 32→40 and Magic to 59 if needed.
   - Take **goldsmith gauntlets** for Smithing and **chaos gauntlets** for cheap Magic training.

3. **Blackjack to 56 Thieving**
   - This unlocks **The Path of Glouphrie**.

4. **Do The Path of Glouphrie**
   - Required for WGS.
   - Gives **20k Slayer XP** and useful Magic/Thieving progress.
   - Do this before grinding Slayer from 59→65.

5. **Use Nieve/Steve Slayer toward 65**
   - You are high enough combat for Nieve/Steve.
   - Burst tasks that naturally stack.
   - Do not force Smoke Burst on bad tasks; use melee/ranged/cannon for normal tasks.
   - Buy or use a **black mask**; work toward Slayer helm and imbue it when practical.

6. **At 65 Slayer, prioritize dust devil tasks**
   - Dust devils require 65 Slayer and DT1 access.
   - Burst them on task. This is the first clean Magic + Slayer training loop.

Checkpoint: 62 Magic unlocks the method; 65 Slayer unlocks the good task.

## Quick Decision Guide

If choosing what to do next:

1. If birdhouses or tree runs are ready, do them.
2. If DT1 is not done, work only on DT1 blockers.
3. If DT1 is done but Magic is under 62, train Magic to 62.
4. If Path of Glouphrie is not done, blackjack to 56 Thieving and complete it.
5. If Slayer is under 65, use Nieve/Steve tasks and burst only good stackable tasks.
6. If Slayer is 65+, burst dust devil tasks and let Magic move toward 67.
7. If Magic is already 67, stop prioritizing Magic and push the remaining WGS skill walls.

## What Not to Do

- Do not train Magic past 57 before DT1 just because the old plan said the Smoke Burst phase starts at 62.
- Do not grind Slayer from 59→65 before DT1; you want Ancients and DT1 Magic XP first.
- Do not delay Path of Glouphrie until after a generic Slayer grind; it gives 20k Slayer XP and is mandatory anyway.
- Do not force burst spells on every Slayer task. Bad burst tasks waste runes and attention.
- Do not let Magic/Slayer distract from Thieving. WGS still requires 72 Thieving, and that remains the biggest wall.
