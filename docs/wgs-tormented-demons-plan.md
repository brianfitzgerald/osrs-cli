## Optimizations (v3)

1. **Finish all 8 in-progress quests first:** Romeo & Juliet, The Lost Tribe, The Giant Dwarf, **The Tourist Trap, Fairytale II, Roving Elves, Monkey Madness I, Doric's Quest**. Low marginal effort, banks QP toward 180.
2. **Fairytale II → fairy rings, done early = travel multiplier.** Most prereqs are far-flung (Trollheim, Rellekka, Tree Gnome, Zanaris); fairy rings cut running time on nearly every later quest. Best single "do-it-first" reorder. Also gives **3,500 Herblore + 2,500 Thieving** (both bottlenecks) + a **2,500 free-choice lamp**.
3. **The Tourist Trap = a player's-choice lamp: 4,650 XP into *two* skills (can stack both into one = 9,300).** Route it into **Agility** (or split Agility/Hunter) — these have essentially no other quest XP. Big lever for the otherwise pure-grind skills.
4. **Family Crest → goldsmith gauntlets *before* finishing the Smithing grind.** Family Crest needs 40 Smithing + 59 Magic (waypoints you pass anyway). Gauntlets give bonus Smithing XP on gold bars → grind 32→40, hit Magic 59, do Family Crest, then **Blast Furnace gold bars 40→55**.
5. **One continuous Thieving grind, not two.** The Feud first (**15,000 Thieving + blackjacking**), then 46→72 in one go; complete Path of Glouphrie when you pass 56 — no context-switch.
6. **Don't plan to boost into WGS** — confirmed not boostable. Eadgar's Ruse Herblore req *is* boostable (minor). Hand in the Sand's 49 Crafting is **not** boostable, so just train the 2 levels (47→49).
7. **Tears of Guthix: unlock at 43 QP, do it every week**, pointed at your lowest bottleneck (Smithing first, then Agility/Hunter/Farming). The only passive XP for the no-quest-XP skills — value compounds with an early start.
8. **Lamp routing for the no-quest-XP skills (Agility/Hunter/Farming):** Tourist Trap (9,300 choice) + Fairytale II (2,500 choice) ≈ **11,800 routable XP** + weekly Tears. Note **Dream Mentor's 15k lamp is combat-skills-only** — it does *not* help the WGS skill walls.

## The three sequencing rules
1. **Unlock the multipliers first** — fairy rings (travel), Blast Furnace + goldsmith gauntlets (Smithing), blackjacking (Thieving), Tears of Guthix (weekly passive).
2. **Do an XP-reward quest immediately before its matching grind**, so the reward starts you higher.
3. **Respect prereq topology** and only train a skill to the bar the *next* quest needs.
