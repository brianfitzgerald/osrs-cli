# Tormented Demons Unlock Plan — Cyberduck242 (v6, single execution plan)

**Goal:** Farm tormented demons. They are gated *only* by **While Guthix Sleeps (WGS)**, a grandmaster quest.

**How to use this doc:** Go top to bottom. **Phase 0** turns on three background skills you keep ticking the whole time. **Phases 1–8** are the active sequence — do each step in order; checkpoints (`▶`) tell you when to pause questing to train a skill to a bar. Desert Treasure I is **baked in as default** (it makes the Magic grind fast and is a huge permanent unlock).

**Data source:** Live stats + quest log via `osrs-cli` (WikiSync upload 2026-05-28). Quest requirements, reward XP, quest points, boostable flags, and training methods are **verified** against the live OSRS Wiki via `osrs-cli wiki`. WGS prereq tree verified against the `While Guthix Sleeps` page.

## Phase 0 — Turn on the background skills (day 1, keep running all plan long)

Set these going immediately; they accrue passively while you do everything below, so they're nearly done by Phase 7.

- **Farming 59→65 — daily tree + fruit-tree runs.** A few minutes per real day. Start *today*.
- **Hunter 52→62 — birdhouse runs.** Passive, ~every 50 min. Needs **Bone Voyage** (Fossil Island access) — do that short quest first if you haven't. *(No-quest alternative: grey chinchompas at 53 in Feldip Hills, needs partial Eagles' Peak — more active.)*
- **Magic 57→67 — AFK combat spells on crabs** during downtime (you don't want to alch). Autocast Fire Blast, or **Fire Bolt + chaos gauntlets** (a Family Crest reward, see Phase 3) for cheaper casts, on sand/ammonite crabs; reset ~every 10 min. *(This converts to fast Smoke Burst in Phase 5.)*
- **Weekly — Tears of Guthix** once you hit 43 QP (early in Phase 1). Point it at your lowest skill (Smithing first, then Hunter/Farming).

## Phase 1 — Quest blitz: bank QP + front-load skill XP

No dedicated grinding yet — just clear quests in this order. They bank QP (toward 180 + unlock Tears at 43), and dump reward XP into your bottlenecks while the bars are low. `[~]` = already in progress.

**1.1 — Quick QP + in-progress cleanup**
- **The Tourist Trap** `[~]` → **9,300 choice XP** — stack it into **Hunter** (least other quest XP).
- **Romeo & Juliet, Doric's Quest, Roving Elves, Monkey Madness I, The Lost Tribe** `[~]` — low effort, banks QP.
- **The Giant Dwarf, Fairytale II** `[~]` — finish for 4 QP (Fairytale II completion wants 57 Herblore — boostable, sip a potion). Optional, do when convenient.

**1.2 — WGS prereq chains (low req, big XP rewards)**
- **Priest in Peril → The Restless Ghost → Nature Spirit** — direct WGS prereqs; Priest in Peril also unlocks Morytania (needed for Desert Treasure I & Creature of Fenkenstrain).
- **Creature of Fenkenstrain** (after Priest in Peril) → +1k Thieving.
- **Garden of Tranquillity** → **5,000 Farming** (Farming's only quest XP).
- **Gertrude's Cat → Icthlarin's Little Helper → A Tail of Two Cats** — direct WGS prereq chain.
- **Rune Mysteries → Enter the Abyss → Temple of the Eye** — direct WGS prereq chain.
- **Recruitment Drive → Wanted!** (via Black Knights' Fortress, Goblin Diplomacy) — direct prereqs; Recruitment Drive gives 1k each Herblore/Agility/Prayer.
- **Demon Slayer, What Lies Below** — WGS-tree / low req.
- **Shield of Arrav, Below Ice Mountain, Temple of Ikov** — Defender-of-Varrock feeders (Temple of Ikov also a Desert Treasure I prereq).

**1.3 — The Glouphrie + Fremennik front-load (do these BEFORE the Magic/Agility/Crafting grinds)**
- **The Grand Tree** → **7,900 Agility + 2,150 Magic** (+18.4k Attack).
- **The Eyes of Glouphrie** (requires Grand Tree) → **12,000 Magic + 2,500 Woodcutting** (+6k RC).
- **Waterfall Quest** + **Tree Gnome Village** — Path of Glouphrie & Desert Treasure I prereqs (Waterfall also big Att/Str).
- **The Fremennik Trials** → **2,812 XP into ~10 skills** (Crafting/WC/Agility/Thieving…); a Lunar prereq.
- **Death Plateau → Troll Stronghold** — combat handles it; gates Eadgar's Ruse.

▶ **Checkpoint:** you should now be ~50–70 QP higher, Tears of Guthix running weekly, and Magic/Agility/WC/Farming already partway up from quest XP.

- **The Feud** (30 Thieving ✓) → **15,000 Thieving** + unlocks **blackjacking**.
- Start the **continuous blackjacking grind** (Pollnivneach): 46–55 Bearded bandits → 55–65 Pollnivnian bandits → 65–72 Menaphite Thugs. This runs across the rest of the plan. ~50k/hr @60, faster up top. Bring dodgy necklaces.

▶ **Checkpoint:** as soon as **Thieving 53** ticks over, you've met the last requirement for Desert Treasure I (Phase 5).

## Phase 3 — The Smithing line (multiplier → fast grind → Defender)

- Get **Magic to 59** (crab sessions; quest XP from Phase 1 already got you most of the way) and **Smithing 32→40** (cheapest route — a little regular smithing).
- **Family Crest** (40 Smithing + 59 Magic) → claim **goldsmith gauntlets** (and pay to also grab **chaos gauntlets** for your Magic crabbing).
- **Blast Furnace gold bars** 40→55 with goldsmith gauntlets ≈ **315k Smithing XP/hr (~1–2 hrs)**. Keldagrim already unlocked.
- **Defender of Varrock** (52 Hunter ✓) → pays back **15k Smithing + 15k Hunter** *after* the req.

## Phase 4 — Herblore, Crafting, and the Lunar/Dream line

- **Eadgar's Ruse** (after Troll Stronghold) → **11,000 Herblore** (~49→54) → then grind **Herblore to 65** by making potions (prayer pots / super energy; ~150k/hr, costs gp).
- **The Hand in the Sand** — train Crafting 47→49 first (not boostable) → **9,000 Crafting + 1k Thieving** → grind **Crafting to 61** by cutting gems (rubies→diamonds). Bank Shilo Village (3,875) + Fremennik (2,812) toward this.
- **Jungle Potion → Shilo Village** (Lunar prereqs; Shilo gives the 3,875 Crafting).
- Train **Woodcutting to 55** (teaks — trivial, mostly covered by Eyes of Glouphrie 2.5k + Fremennik 2.8k).
- **Lunar Diplomacy** (Crafting 61, Magic 65, WC 55, Mining 60 ✓ + Fremennik Trials/Lost City/Rune Mysteries/Shilo) → **5,000 Magic + 5,000 Runecraft** + Lunar spellbook.
- **Dream Mentor** (after Lunar + Eadgar's; 85 combat ✓) → **10,000 Magic** + 15k HP + a 15k *combat-only* lamp.

> Magic note: train crabs only up to **62** here — don't grind Magic past that with crabs, because Phase 5 makes it fast.

## Phase 5 — Desert Treasure I (default) → fast Magic

**Trigger:** Thieving ≥ 53 (from Phase 2) and Magic ≥ 62.

- **The Dig Site** — the only off-WGS-path prereq (trivial reqs, ~20–30 min). Every other DT1 prereq (Temple of Ikov, Tourist Trap, Troll Stronghold, Priest in Peril, Waterfall Quest) is already done above.
- **Desert Treasure I** (Long, master; four bosses — bring water spells/ice gloves for Fareed, a Guthix balance / blessed setup for Dessous, prayer pots) → **Ancient Magicks** + **+3 QP** (Dig Site +2).
- Switch Magic training to **Smoke Burst on a Slayer task** (62+): fast Magic **+ Slayer** XP, breaks even on cost. Finish **Magic to 67** this way.

## Phase 7 — Close the remaining walls

Background skills (Farming/Hunter/Magic) should be done or nearly so. Finish the rest tallest-last so quest XP + weekly Tears chip in first:

- **Thieving → 72** (keep blackjacking Menaphite Thugs — your biggest single sink at ~1.4M XP).
- **Herblore → 65**, **Crafting → 61** (if not already from Phase 4).
- **Agility → 66** — Wilderness Agility Course (fastest, PK risk) or Seers' Rooftop (safe); grab marks of grace for graceful.
- **Hunter → 62**, **Farming → 65**, **Magic → 67**, **Woodcutting → 55** — finish off whatever the background runs haven't covered.

## Appendix C — QP gate & after the unlock

- **QP gate:** WGS needs 180. The WGS-tree chain + Desert Treasure I (3) + Dig Site (2) + Giant Dwarf/Fairytale II (4) realistically adds ~55–75 QP on top of your current total. Verify live before the final step.
- **After the unlock:** your combat/Prayer (56) is low for efficient TD farming — aim for **70+ Prayer** (protection prayers), better gear, and learn their elemental-shield cycling before committing to a grind. Dream Mentor's combat lamp + questing combat XP help along the way.
