### Solo ToA setup

This is for learning at raid level 0. Beneath Cursed Sands is the only raid access quest. The current 76 Attack, 76 Strength, 73 Defence, 78 Hitpoints, 78 Ranged, 67 Magic, and 70 Prayer clear most of the Wiki's suggested levels. Magic is three levels below its suggested 70. Piety is unlocked. A fire cape, Rigour, Augury, and 75 Magic are not entry requirements. [ToA strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Tombs_of_Amascut/Strategies)

| Style | Wear or carry | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared | Helm of Neitiznot, fury, brimstone ring, regen bracelet, blessed d'hide body and chaps; use the blessed d'hide boots | Keep these equipped to save inventory space. Check the bank for the items shown only in the DPS link. |
| Melee | Dragon sword, keris partisan from Beneath Cursed Sands, dragon defender if owned, dragon dagger for special attacks | Use keris at Kephri, dragon sword on other stab targets, and dragon dagger on the Warden core. Get a dragon defender before repeated runs if missing; 76 Attack + 76 Strength meets the Warriors' Guild entry rule. Use Piety. |
| Ranged | Charged toxic blowpipe, Ava's accumulator, enough darts and scales | Use on Zebak, Ranged phases, and low-defence targets. Use Eagle Eye. If Zebak or Wardens feels slow, add a rune crossbow with ruby bolts (e). |
| Magic | Charged warped sceptre, mystic robe top and bottom | Swap both robes to remove the blessed d'hide Magic attack penalty. Use at Akkha and Wardens with Mystic Might. The sceptre needs 62 Magic and uses 2 chaos runes and 5 earth runes per charge. Add mystic boots if Magic accuracy is poor. |
| Utility | Rune pickaxe or better | Use in the Path of Het; 68 Mining meets the requirement. |

The owned blowpipe, blessed d'hide, Neitiznot helm, and fury cover the main setup. Confirm the ring and bracelet from the shared DPS link in the bank. Buy or check the dragon sword, warped sceptre and its charges, mystic top and bottom, dragon dagger, and a pickaxe. Check for a dragon defender. If the upgraded Iban's staff is already owned, it is another Magic option at 67 Magic. It hits harder per cast but needs fire and death runes in the inventory. The warped sceptre saves those slots. The 2026-09-24 instant-buy prices were about 98k gp for a dragon sword and 89k gp for an uncharged warped sceptre. Keep most of the visible 3.88m cash for supplies. [Warped sceptre](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Warped_sceptre) · [Iban's staff](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Iban%27s_staff)

For the first inventory, take one super combat potion, one ranging potion, four Saradomin brews, four super restores, a pickaxe, the melee and Magic switches, and food in the remaining slots. Adjust brews and restores after the first run. Use an antipoison before entry or bring one if poison is a problem. Leave attempt limits and time limits off. Learn Zebak -> Kephri -> Akkha -> Ba-Ba. Take Power for smelling salts at the first supply choice, then Life for the final fight. [ToA strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Tombs_of_Amascut/Strategies)

Treat raid level 0 as practice, not the main cash source. After clean Entry Mode runs, train to 75 Magic for a trident, then add a crossbow if Ranged bosses are slow. Save for an Osmumten's fang when moving toward raid level 150. Skip a Zamorakian hasta for the first runs: wielding it needs the Smithing part of Barbarian Training, which depends on Tai Bwo Wannai Trio. [Hasta requirement](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zamorakian_hasta)
